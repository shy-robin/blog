The Soft UI Trend

A subtle approach to design

Neumorphism is a design trend that has gained popularity for its soft, extruded-plastic look. It's a style that sits somewhere between skeuomorphism and flat design, creating a tactile experience for the user.

The key is the use of subtle shadows and highlights to create the illusion of depth. Elements appear to be pushed through the background, creating a seamless, three-dimensional interface.
