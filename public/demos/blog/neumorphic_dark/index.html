Neumorphic Dark

Into the Shadows

A new perspective on soft UI

Neumorphism isn't just for light backgrounds. This demo explores how the same principles of soft, extruded UIs can be applied to a dark theme, creating a moody and sophisticated aesthetic.

The key is to adjust the shadow colors. Instead of light and dark gray shadows, we use darker shades for the concave effect and slightly lighter shades for the convex highlights.
